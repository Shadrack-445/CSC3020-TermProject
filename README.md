Project Title - Barber Shop & Salon Platform
Two customers: the business owner and the person receiving the service. For the business, it's a marketing platform and business management platform, with financial
stats and possibly advice. For the consumer, it's an easy way to find barber shops and salons near you; you can leave reviews, possibly earn credits for discounted service. etc.
